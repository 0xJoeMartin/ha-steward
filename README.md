# Steward

**A proactive caretaker for Home Assistant.**

Steward is a Home Assistant add-on that watches your home on its own, notices what needs attention, runs small
experiments to learn how your house behaves, and *proposes* changes (automations, sensors, rules) for you to
approve from your phone, your dashboard or by voice. It never acts on its own without your approval.

**Status:** early development. Steward currently runs privately in one home (where it is still called "Jarvis").
The add-on itself is not published yet.
